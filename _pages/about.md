I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) postdoctoral fellow in mathematics at the University of Jyväskylä in Finland, where I work with [Tuomas Orponen](https://sites.google.com/view/tuomaths/). My main research interests lie in geometry and analysis, particularly in questions concerning fractal sets and measures.  

Fractals are intricate and often beautiful mathematical objects which exhibit complexity over a wide range of scales. They often arise naturally through dynamical processes, for example from conformal or non-conformal iterated function systems. I am especially interested in the rate of decay of the Fourier transforms of stationary measures for such systems; this is the subject of my MSCA fellowship 'Fourier Decay for Nonlinear Fractal Measures.' Another strand of my research explores different notions of dimension, such as the [intermediate dimensions](https://amlan-banaji.github.io/files/MicrothesisLMS.pdf), which provide refined information about the global and local scaling properties of sets and measures. I enjoy finding connections between these topics and other areas of mathematics such as number theory, probability, and dynamical systems.  

From 2023 to 2025, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Loughborough University, working with [Simon Baker](https://simonbakermaths.wordpress.com/). I completed my PhD at the University of St Andrews in 2023, under the supervision of [Jonathan Fraser](https://jonathan-fraser.github.io/homepage/) and [Kenneth Falconer](https://kennethfalconer.github.io/index.html). Before this, I completed my undergraduate degree in mathematics at the University of Cambridge.
